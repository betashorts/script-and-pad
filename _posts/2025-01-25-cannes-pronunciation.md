<h2>Common Mispronunciations</h2>

<p>The name “Cannes” is notoriously tricky for non-French speakers. Here are the most common mistakes people make:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>“Canes”</strong> – Like the walking stick or candy cane.</li>
  <li><strong>“Cans”</strong> – Like tin cans. Close, but not quite right.</li>
  <li><strong>“Cons”</strong> – This one might sound closer, but it’s not the correct pronunciation.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to Pronounce “Cannes” Correctly</h2>

<p>The correct way to pronounce “Cannes” is <strong>“Kan”</strong> (like the English word “can” but with a subtle French twist). The “s” at the end is silent. It’s a simple, one-syllable word, pronounced with a slight nasal tone, typical of French phonetics.</p>

<h3>Quick Tips for Getting It Right</h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Simplify:</strong> It’s just one syllable—“Kan.”</li>
  <li><strong>Silent “S”:</strong> French words often drop the ending “s.”</li>
  <li><strong>Practice the Accent:</strong> A slight nasal tone gives your pronunciation authenticity.</li>
</ul>
