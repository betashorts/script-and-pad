<p>Cannes is more than just the glitz and glamour of its world-famous film festival. Nestled on the French Riviera, this beautiful city offers a mix of luxury, history, and charm. Whether you’re visiting for business, leisure, or a bit of both, here’s how to make the most of your time in Cannes.</p>

<h2>1. Stroll Along La Croisette</h2>

<p>No visit to Cannes is complete without a walk along La Croisette, the city’s iconic beachfront promenade. Lined with luxury hotels, upscale boutiques, and stunning views of the Mediterranean, it’s the perfect spot to soak up the glamour Cannes is famous for.</p>

<h3>What to Do on La Croisette:</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Shop at designer stores like Chanel and Gucci.</li>
  <li>Relax on the sandy beaches or rent a private cabana.</li>
  <li>Enjoy fine dining at one of the many seaside restaurants.</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. Explore Le Suquet – The Old Town</h2>

<p>For a change of pace, head to Le Suquet, Cannes’ historic Old Town. This charming neighborhood is a maze of narrow cobblestone streets, quaint cafes, and local shops.</p>

<h3>Highlights of Le Suquet:</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Climb up to the <strong>Église Notre-Dame d'Espérance</strong> for panoramic views of the city and coastline.</li>
  <li>Wander through the <strong>Marché Forville</strong>, a vibrant market offering fresh produce, cheeses, and local specialties.</li>
  <li>Enjoy authentic French cuisine in one of the cozy bistros.</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. Visit the Palais des Festivals</h2>

<p>The <strong>Palais des Festivals et des Congrès</strong> is where the magic of the Cannes Film Festival happens. Even if you’re not visiting during the festival, you can take a guided tour or snap a photo on the famous red carpet.</p>

<h2>4. Take a Boat Trip to the Lérins Islands</h2>

<p>Just a short boat ride from Cannes, the <strong>Lérins Islands</strong> are a hidden gem. These serene islands are perfect for a day of exploration and relaxation.</p>

<h3>Must-See Spots on the Lérins Islands:</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Île Sainte-Marguerite:</strong> Home to the Fort Royal, where the mysterious Man in the Iron Mask was imprisoned.</li>
  <li><strong>Île Saint-Honorat:</strong> Known for its peaceful monastery and local wine production.</li>
</ul>

<h2>5. Indulge in Cannes’ Culinary Scene</h2>

<p>Cannes is a haven for food lovers. From Michelin-starred restaurants to casual eateries, the city offers a wide range of culinary delights.</p>

<h3>What to Try:</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Seafood:</strong> Freshly caught fish and shellfish are a must.</li>
  <li><strong>Local Wines:</strong> Enjoy a glass of rosé from Provence.</li>
  <li><strong>Pastries:</strong> Indulge in buttery croissants and decadent desserts.</li>
</ul>

<h2>6. Relax at Cannes Beaches</h2>

<p>Cannes boasts some of the most beautiful beaches on the French Riviera. Whether you prefer public beaches or private luxury spots, there’s something for everyone.</p>
