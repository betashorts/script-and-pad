Why Script & Pad

Short films are where great filmmakers begin.

Short films are where filmmakers learn their craft, take risks, and discover their voice. But for too long, the tools and knowledge needed to make great shorts have been gated behind expensive software and film school tuition.

Script and Pad exists to change that. We build free, accessible tools — and pair them with honest, practical guides — so that anyone with a story to tell can actually make their film.

Whether it's your first short or your tenth, we've got you.
